# 📊 HR Analytics: Employee Attrition & Retention Insights

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze workforce turnover dynamics, uncover key retention drivers, and identify high-attrition employee cohorts across departments, tenure tiers, and job roles.

## 🔍 Key Insights & Breakdown

* **Department Vulnerability:** 
  * **Research & Development** accounts for the largest share of departures with **133 exits (56%)**[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
  * **Sales** follows with **92 exits (39%)**[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
  * **Human Resources** records **12 exits (5%)**[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
* **Experience & Tenure Curve:** Attrition peaks within the first 1 to 3 years at the company, tapering off significantly after year 5[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
* **Age Demographics:** Highest turnover occurs in the **26–35** age bracket, followed by **18–25**, indicating early-to-mid career transition vulnerability[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
* **Job Roles Most Impacted:** Laboratory Technicians (62), Sales Executives (57), and Research Scientists (47) represent the primary attrition volume[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
* **Hierarchy Level:** **Entry Level (148 exits)** experiences vastly higher turnover than Junior (52), Senior (32), and Executive (5) tiers[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
* **Gender Distribution:** Male employee exits (150) outnumber female employee exits (87)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)
* **ETL & Data Cleaning:** Power Query (handling nulls, restructuring dimensions, type transformation)
* **Analytical Modeling:** DAX measures (calculating attrition rates, dynamic counts, tenure bins)
* **Visual Formats:** Donut charts, Area trend lines, Horizontal bar rankings, and KPI Summary Cards[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span)

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Onboarding & Early Retention Programs:** Target the 0–3 year tenure window with structured mentorship to stem the peak departure rate[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).
2. **Career Pathing for Entry-Level Roles:** Over 60% of attrition stems from entry-level positions[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span); establishing transparent promotion pathways will reduce early exits.
3. **Targeted Interventions in R&D:** Conduct focused stay-interviews within Technical and Laboratory roles to diagnose workflow and compensation friction points[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).
